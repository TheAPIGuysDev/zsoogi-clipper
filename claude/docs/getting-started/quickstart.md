# Quick Start Guide

Get capturing content in 5 minutes! This guide assumes you've already [installed Zsoogi Clipper](installation.md).

---

## Step 1: Install the Bookmarklet (2 minutes)

### 1.1 Show Your Bookmarks Bar

If your bookmarks bar isn't visible:

- **Chrome/Edge:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+B` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+B` (Mac)
- **Firefox:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+B` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+B` (Mac)
- **Safari:** View → Show Favorites Bar

### 1.2 Navigate to Installation Page

In your WordPress admin:
1. Click **Zsoogi Clips** in the left menu
2. Click **Grab Zsoogi** submenu
3. You'll see a big button with your site name

### 1.3 Drag the Bookmarklet

Drag the bookmarklet button to your bookmarks bar. It should say something like:
```
🔖 YourSite ZsoogiClips
```

!!! success "Bookmarklet Installed!"
    You only need to do this once. The bookmarklet will work on any website you visit.

---

## Step 2: Capture Your First Clip (1 minute)

### 2.1 Visit Any Webpage

Open a new tab and go to any article, blog post, or YouTube video you want to capture.

**Try this example:**
- Visit [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research)
- Or any YouTube video with captions/transcript

### 2.2 Select Text (Optional)

Highlight any text you want to capture. This will become a blockquote in your clip.

!!! tip "Pro Tip"
    You can capture without selecting text - it will just capture the URL, title, and image.

### 2.3 Click the Bookmarklet

Click the bookmarklet in your bookmarks bar.

**What happens:**
1. A new window/tab opens
2. WordPress new post editor appears
3. Content is pre-filled:
   - Selected text (as blockquote)
   - Source citation with link
   - Notes section ready for your commentary

### 2.4 Add Your Notes & Publish

1. Scroll to the "Notes" section
2. Add your commentary, thoughts, or analysis
3. Click **Publish** (or **Save Draft** for later)

!!! success "First Clip Created!"
    Your clip is now saved in **Zsoogi Clips → All Zsoogi Clips**

---

## Step 3: Organize Your Clips (1 minute)

### 3.1 View All Clips

Go to **Zsoogi Clips → All Zsoogi Clips** to see your captured content.

### 3.2 Add Taxonomies

Click **Edit** on any clip and use the "Zsoogi Type" taxonomy to organize:
- Research
- Articles
- Videos
- Case Studies
- etc.

!!! info "Premium Feature"
    Premium and Enterprise users can create unlimited custom taxonomies beyond "Zsoogi Type".

### 3.3 Set Featured Images

Featured images are auto-set if "Auto-Featured Image" is enabled in settings. You can change them anytime in the post editor.

---

## Step 4: Configure Settings (1 minute)

Go to **Zsoogi Clips → Settings** to customize:

### Basic Settings

**Auto-Set Featured Image**
- ✅ Recommended: Keep this ON
- Automatically sets captured image as featured image

**Include Page Metadata**
- Optional: Shows capture date/time in content
- Useful for tracking when research was gathered

## Common Workflows

### Academic Research

1. Visit article or PDF
2. Select key quotes
3. Click bookmarklet
4. Add analysis in Notes section
5. Tag with course/topic
6. Publish for later reference

### Content Creation Research

1. Browse competitor content
2. Capture interesting ideas (don't copy!)
3. Add your unique angle in Notes
4. Organize by content topic
5. Reference when writing

### YouTube Video Research

**Free Version:**
- Captures clean title and thumbnail
- Links to video

**Premium Version:**
- Also captures full transcript
- Searchable transcript text
- Timestamp links

## Tips & Tricks

### Bookmarklet Not Working?

!!! warning "Popup Blocked?"
    If nothing happens, check if your browser blocked the popup. Click "Always allow popups from [yoursite.com]" in the address bar.

### Capture Without Selecting Text

You don't need to select text! Click the bookmarklet without selection to just capture URL, title, and image.

### Multiple Images

Free version captures the first meaningful image. Premium can capture multiple images as a gallery.

### Edit After Capture

The bookmarklet just pre-fills content. You can edit everything before publishing:
- Change the title
- Edit blockquotes
- Add more content
- Remove sections you don't want

### Search Your Clips

Use WordPress search to find clips by:
- Title keywords
- Content
- Source URL
- Tags/taxonomies
